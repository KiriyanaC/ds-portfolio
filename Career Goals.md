## Current DS Career Goals

My data science career goals end with a statistical career after graduating with a master’s degree in statistics from Oregon State University. The shorter-term goals that lead to that include gaining understanding of the field through online research and interviews, gaining experience through research positions and internships, learning coding, joining organizations, and finishing my bachelor’s degree before my master’s. Firstly, this 2027 Winter Term at OSU, I plan on better understanding the statistician career as well as adjacent ones. Online research, visiting career fairs, and job-shadowing statisticians and similar people will help understand the daily tasks, requirements, and differing roles in different sectors for such a career. In the springtime, I hope to have a research position with URSA Engage to gain experience and understanding of doing statistics outside of coursework. In the summertime, I hope to have an internship that uses statistics to put experience on my resume. Throughout this year, I hope to learn how to code in multiple programs, mainly through coursework, but additionally through online sources and research if I find the time or opportunity. After solidifying my commitment to gaining a statistics career, I hope to join a statistical or data science organization for resources in networking, mentoring, and opportunities.

#### Reflection on Statistician Job Understanding Goal

Focusing in on the goal for better understanding the statistician career, the main things I hope to learn about are the daily tasks, the most tedious parts of the job, the work-life balance, how much artificial intelligence is used and what kind, and how much coding I will have to learn. I want to prepare myself for unexpected downsides before I fully commit to the coursework, degrees, and work outside of education for this kind of career. The AI connects to my values and how I’m unsure about working with it, even though it seems like many job postings list it. Finding differences between data analyst and statistician roles and what tools they use and how much AI is used is a key thing for me and I hope to find out more. I also used to vow that I will never code for a job, and now I’m realizing I will have to, so researching how easy or difficult it really is will be crucial to see if this career is worth it.
