In the springtime, I hope to have a research position with URSA Engage to gain experience and understanding of doing statistics outside of coursework. In the summertime, I hope to have an internship that uses statistics to put experience on my resume. Throughout this year, I hope to learn how to code in multiple programs, mainly through coursework, but additionally through online sources and research if I find the time or opportunity. After solidifying my commitment to gaining a statistics career, I hope to join a statistical or data science organization for resources in networking, mentoring, and opportunities.
