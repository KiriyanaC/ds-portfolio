## "Does Surveyor Gender Affect Opinions on Abortion"
A statistical experiment and survey titled, "Does Surveyor Gender Affect Opinions on Abortion,” was presented for an AP Statistics spring-term class of 2024. With a partner, I designed and conducted an experiment to use statistical design and testing skills to research the opinions of the Clackamas Community College students. We were researching this specific population on their possible biases regarding abortion and genders who talk about them. The general null hypothesis was that there was that the surveyor’s gender had no effect on the answer to the abortion question inside the survey, and the alternative was that there was an effect. Data was collected via a partially blinded survey to reduce bias and ensure that the conditions of our samples were random, independent, and adequate sizes. We ran inference procedures such as a test and a confidence interval for a difference of proportions as well as chi-square test for homogeneity. Google Sheets was used to generate test statistics and p-values from the collected data. The overall conclusion from the tests was that there was no statistically significant difference in responses from our population. The survey was presented and communicated to our class with introduction, methodology, results, discussion, and conclusion sections.
